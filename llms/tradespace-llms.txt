# Tradespace

> AI-based IP management platform that helps legal teams capture inventions, file patents, manage portfolios, and commercialize intellectual property without law firm overhead.

Tradespace combines AI tooling, IP management software, and on-demand patent attorneys into a single platform. It serves corporations, universities, startups, law firms, and government agencies. The platform covers the full IP lifecycle: invention harvesting, patent filing and prosecution, portfolio management, and licensing.

## Platform

- [Create](https://tradespace.io/platform/create/): Invention harvesting and disclosure; integrates with Slack, Gmail, and Notion
- [Protect](https://tradespace.io/platform/protect/): AI-assisted patent filing with senior attorneys; flat-fee prosecution
- [Manage](https://tradespace.io/platform/manage/): Portfolio management, maintenance decisions, and board-ready reporting
- [Commercialize](https://tradespace.io/platform/commercialize/): Licensing campaign tools, claims charts, and licensee identification
